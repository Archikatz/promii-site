ArchiKatz started as an art universe: architect cats, sacred geometry, gems and neon cities. Over the years it became an encyclopedia of ideas, characters and possible books. The problem with an encyclopedia is that it never ends. This post is about how I'm turning that universe into a **publishing imprint** with a plan to actually publish.

## The problem: too many ways to organize it

When I reviewed all the material I found **four different architectures** competing with each other: a saga in seven volumes, a second volume with ten collections, a "bible" of the universe in five books, and the idea of a publishing imprint. They all made sense, but together they were paralyzing: every new idea fit in three places at once.

The decision was to pick a single structure that contains the others: **an imprint with five collections**.

## The five collections

1. **Children's and STEM**: books to learn science, geometry and architecture through play.
2. **Fulfillment**: wellbeing and personal growth.
3. **Workbooks**: practical workbooks.
4. **Fiction and universes**: this is where ArchiKatz lives, with its characters and worlds.
5. **Professional development**: guides for work, like the ones in the Academy.

ArchiKatz becomes a collection inside the imprint, not the whole imprint. That gives it a clear place and leaves room for the rest.

## Finish before you grow

The plan has three phases:

- **Phase 0, decisions.** Lock the structure, the master book format and the prices. Without this, every book reopens the same debates.
- **Phase 1, one single book.** Publish the first ArchiKatz book, about **sacred geometry**, from start to finish: write it, lay it out and put it on sale.
- **Phase 2, constitute the imprint.** With one book published and the process proven, formalize the imprint and open the other collections.

## How it will be published

- **Master format.** One layout template for every book, with accessibility criteria (easy reading and adapted versions).
- **Distribution.** Ebooks and print-on-demand on Amazon KDP, digital products on Hotmart and local print-on-demand.
- **Tiered pricing.** A very low-cost entry to discover the universe and full editions at book prices.
- **Identity.** The gem palette from the color system: obsidian, gold, sapphire and amethyst. It's the same family the Obsidian style of this site uses.

## What I learned

A creative universe can grow forever. A publishing catalog can't: it needs finished books. The lesson for any big personal project is the same: **pick one structure, finish one piece, then grow**.
