Every course, mentorship and project I do leaves something useful, but for a long time those lessons stayed in notes I never opened again. **Promii Academy** was born to fix that: turning what I learn into strategies I can apply, teach and reuse.

## From notes to playbooks

The rule is simple: a lesson isn't finished until it becomes a **playbook**, a card with a goal, the steps, the tools and how to tell if it worked. Each playbook has a code so it's quick to find:

- **MKT**: marketing strategies. For example, a *website-first* strategy (the website at the center of everything), direct-to-consumer performance, B2B copywriting, B2B webinars, webinar with a lead magnet, and positioning.
- **KAM**: key account management and B2B sales. For example, LinkedIn outreach to dormant contacts, an AI appointment setter connected to the CRM, CRM architecture, staged rollouts, multichannel nurturing, lead scoring, and follow-up and closing guides.

## The five layers

The academy is organized in layers, from the most general to the most concrete:

1. **Sources.** The courses and programs the knowledge comes from.
2. **Strategy library.** The MKT and KAM playbooks.
3. **Procedures (SOPs).** How each strategy is executed step by step, with templates.
4. **Tools.** Ticket templates, master documents and automations that go with each procedure.
5. **Evidence.** Projects and results where the strategy was actually used.

## Why it works

- **A playbook can be reused.** When I start a new project, I don't start from zero: I find the playbook, adapt it and apply it.
- **It can be taught.** The same format I use for myself works to explain to someone else how to do it.
- **It can be measured.** Each card says how to tell if it worked, so I can improve it every time I use it.
