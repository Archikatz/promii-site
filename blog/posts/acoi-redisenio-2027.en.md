# Field case: ACOI web redesign 2027

How we moved the Colombian Association of Immunology site from a static Wix page to an immersive **liquid glass** proposal—without inventing institutional copy, and reusing the existing brand archive.

## The problem

The public site at [inmunoacoi.com](https://www.inmunoacoi.com/) works, but the visual experience and information architecture do not match the scientific community or the documented brand system (2023 manual, digital and print logos).

## The approach

1. **Validated content:** news, membership tiers, 2025 dues, and contact from the official site and public ACOI communications.
2. **Faithful brand:** palette `#47744B` / `#95C35D`, cellular mark (three upper cells, core, side cells).
3. **Liquid glass:** logo “cells” become interactive drops with spring physics—same visual family as [promii.co/home](https://promii.co/home/), tuned to ACOI.
4. **Safe preview:** hosted at [promii.co/acoi/](https://promii.co/acoi/) with `noindex` until the client approves and the domain is ready.

## What to open

- Hero with logo drops (drag / reassemble).
- News, events, association, membership, links.
- Field-work badge (Studio Brian Zuleta).
